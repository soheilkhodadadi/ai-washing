\caption{Variable definitions: disclosure and credibility measures}
\label{tab:appendix_var_defs}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.17\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.20\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
\hline
Group & Variable & Definition \\
\hline
Disclosure counts & Total AI sentences & Count of classified AI-related sentences in the firm's annual filing for year $t$. \\
Disclosure counts & Actionable AI sentences & Count of AI sentences labeled actionable in year $t$. \\
Disclosure counts & Speculative AI sentences & Count of AI sentences labeled speculative in year $t$. \\
Disclosure counts & Irrelevant AI sentences & Count of AI sentences labeled irrelevant in year $t$. \\
Disclosure composition & AI Focus & Broad AI-disclosure intensity, measured as $\log(1 + \text{total AI sentences})$. \\
Disclosure composition & Actionable share & Actionable AI sentences divided by the sum of actionable and speculative AI sentences in the firm-year. \\
Disclosure composition & Speculative share & Speculative AI sentences divided by the sum of actionable and speculative AI sentences in the firm-year. \\
Disclosure composition & CredAI & Credibility score that rises with actionable content and falls with speculative content, computed as the standardized actionable count minus the standardized speculative count. \\
Disclosure composition & AS ratio & $\log(1 + A/(1+S))$, where $A$ and $S$ are actionable and speculative counts; higher values mean more actionable relative to speculative disclosure. \\
Credibility construct & PatentMismatch & Indicator equal to one for AI-talking firm-years that are both low-credibility in disclosure and weak in contemporaneous AI patent grants relative to the industry-year mean. \\
Credibility construct & LowCredibility & Indicator equal to one for AI-talking firm-years with low disclosure credibility, defined as bottom yearly quartile of the AS ratio or top yearly quartile of speculative share. \\
Credibility construct & ApplicationMismatch & Indicator equal to one for AI-talking firm-years that satisfy the low-credibility disclosure condition and are weak in contemporaneous AI patent applications relative to the industry-year benchmark. \\
Credibility construct & Strict patent mismatch & Indicator equal to one for AI-talking firm-years that satisfy both language-side low-credibility conditions and are weak in contemporaneous AI patent grants relative to the industry-year benchmark. \\
Credibility construct & Weak patent only & Indicator equal to one for firm-years with contemporaneous AI patent grants below the industry-year benchmark, before imposing the disclosure-credibility condition. \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\caption*{\footnotesize \textit{Note.} This table defines the disclosure-count, disclosure-composition, and credibility-construct variables used in the analysis. All variables are measured on the annual sample of firms that mention AI at least once during 2016--2025.}
\end{table}
